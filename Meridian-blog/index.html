Poetry

Short lines, long echoes

i.

the desert does not / apologize for / what it withholds —
neither will I.

ii.

every meridian / is a promise / the earth makes / to itself.

iii.

silence is not / empty — it is / a room I have not / finished furnishing.

“

Not all who wander are lost, but most of us are, and that is where the good notes come from.

”

— Field notes, Meridian Vol. III

About the Journal

Written between destinations

Meridian began as a set of notebooks carried across mountains, server rooms, and quiet libraries. It stayed a journal because the terrain kept changing — a ridge line one week, a philosophy seminar the next, a satellite downlink after that.

Every entry is filed under a waypoint, not a genre. The territory is the same whether you're reading about a glacier or a theory of mind: attention, carefully spent.
